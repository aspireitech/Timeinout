Basic

What is ClockInOut?

A sign-in and sign-out kiosk for any front door. People tap their name on a tablet; owners see who is in, who left, who picked up whom, and get attendance and hours reports by email. Built for daycares, tutoring centers, schools, clinics, dental offices, offices, gyms and crews.

What devices do I need?

Any tablet or phone with a web browser (iPad, Android tablet, spare smartphone). Nothing to install, no card reader, no special hardware. Tip: add the page to the tablet's home screen so it opens like an app.

How do I start? Is there a free trial?

Yes: 7 days free, no credit card. Go to clockinoutapp.com/signup, enter your business name, industry, state, your name, email and a password. Your portal is ready in about a minute.

How does a person sign in at the kiosk?
  1. Tap the right tile (Student, Teacher / Staff, Visitor, Material pickup).
  2. Type the first letters of the name and tap it.
  3. Students: choose who is with them (a listed parent, Authorized relative, or Self).
  4. Tap Sign in or Sign out. Done.
What is "Authorized relative"?

When someone not on the list picks up (for example Grandma), she taps Authorized relative, types her name and chooses the relationship. It is saved with the sign-out, so your record shows exactly who left with the child. The owner can switch this option off in Branding & settings.

What does "Self" mean?

For adults and older students with no guardian on file: they sign themselves in and out.

Where do I see who is in right now?

Admin → Dashboard (who is on site, staff on site, pickups) or Today's attendance for the full list with times.

How do I get reports?

Admin → Reports: daily, weekly, monthly or custom range, as PDF, CSV or email. Scheduled reports arrive automatically at 7:00 local time (daily = yesterday, weekly on your chosen weekday, monthly on the 1st), only the ones ticked in settings.

How do I add my students or staff?

One by one with the Add button on Students & guardians or Teachers & staff, or all at once with Import on that list (Excel or CSV).

Can the kiosk show my logo and colors?

Yes: Admin → Branding & settings → logo, main and accent colors, welcome title and message.

What happens when the free trial ends?

The admin portal opens only Billing (and Help) until a plan is chosen. The kiosk keeps working 7 more days so families are not turned away, then pauses. Nothing is deleted.

How do I pay?

Admin → Billing → choose monthly or yearly → choose a plan → pay by card (Stripe, renews automatically). If card payment isn't available, "Choose plan" sends a request and you receive an invoice.

How do I change my card or cancel?

Admin → Billing → Manage card & plan opens the secure Stripe page where you update the card, switch plan or cancel.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

There is no self-service reset, on purpose. Staff: ask your admin (Admin → Users → set a new password). Owner: contact ClockInOut support; the provider resets it and you choose a new one at first sign-in.

Why do I get a 6-digit code when I sign in as admin?

It is an extra security check sent to your email. It expires after 10 minutes. If you didn't try to sign in, use the "I did not request this" link in that email.

Is my data safe?

Yes: HTTPS only; phone numbers and emails are encrypted; each customer's data is kept apart; admin sign-ins need an emailed code; every change is recorded in an audit log; daily backups. See page 9, Security.

Intermediate

A child signed in but nobody signed them out. Can they sign in tomorrow?

Yes. Every day starts fresh, so tomorrow they can sign in normally. Yesterday's missing sign-out shows in Reports under "Signed in, never signed out" so you can follow up.

Can someone enter a sign-in for an earlier day?

Only as far back as the admin allows: Branding & settings → Allow entries for past days (0–30 days; default 7). Every such entry is recorded with who made it.

Which files can I import, and how?
  1. Open the list (Students or Teachers & staff) → Import.
  2. Upload .xlsx or .csv (max 5 MB).
  3. Match your columns to the fields once; the match is remembered.
  4. Check the preview → Import.
I imported into the wrong list. Can I undo?

Yes, within 24 hours: open that list → Undo this import. People who already have attendance are kept. Then import again from the correct list.

I reached my plan's people limit. What now?

You see a message when adding people. Upgrade in Admin → Billing (Business allows 500, Enterprise unlimited) or mark people who left as inactive. Signing in and out is never blocked by limits.

How do I add another admin or kiosk login?

Admin → Users → name, role (Admin or Staff/kiosk only), user ID or email, password (or let the app create one) → optionally email the sign-in details. Starter allows 3 logins.

How do I change when reports arrive or who gets them?

Admin → Branding & settings (report section): recipients, report hour, weekday for the weekly report, which reports (daily/weekly/monthly) and attachments (PDF/CSV).

How do visitors sign in?

Turn on the Visitor tile (Admin → Kiosk tiles). Visitors enter name, company and who they visit, and sign out from the list of current visitors.

What are pickup materials?

Items handed out at the kiosk (homework folders, books, worksheets, tools…). Admin → Pickup materials to add or hide items; each pickup is logged with who took it and when.

Where can I see everything that happened?

Admin → Activity log (sign-ins and sign-outs) and Audit log (every change, import, export and sign-in). You (provider) have the audit log for all portals in /super → Audit log.

(Provider) How do I know where a new customer came from?

/super → Subscribers shows a "via …" badge (facebook, linkedin, outreach, email…). Use tracked links like clockinoutapp.com/facebook or /for/healthcare?src=newsletter.

(Provider) A customer's own address shows "can't provide a secure connection".

Its subdomain isn't created yet. hPanel → Domains → Subdomains → create name with custom folder public_html, then make sure SSL is issued. Meanwhile it works at clockinoutapp.com/s/name. Links switch automatically within an hour.

(Provider) A lead replied to my outreach email. What do I do?

/super → Prospects → find the lead → set Replied → Save (stops the follow-ups) → answer from contact@.

(Provider) Why are my prospects still "Queued"?
  1. The hourly cron job must exist (hPanel → Cron Jobs).
  2. Sending must be ticked in /super → Prospects, with your name and postal address saved.
  3. Emails only go out in each lead's business hours (weekdays) and at most 4 per hour / 20 per day, so a backlog clears over a few days.
(Provider) When exactly are outreach emails sent?

In the lead's own time zone (from their state): Tue–Thu 9–12 and 1–3, Monday 10–12 and 1–3, Friday 9–12. Never at lunch, evenings or weekends. Follow-up after 4 days, last note 5 days later.

(Provider) How do I add my own leads?

/super → Prospects → Add prospects, one per line: Organization, First name, Email, City, State, Type, Website, Phone. Type decides which email they get (clinic, dental, daycare, tutoring, school, office, gym, construction, retail, restaurant, other).

(Provider) Someone asked not to be contacted.

Set them to Unsubscribed (or they click the unsubscribe link themselves). They are never emailed again and can't be re-added.

(Provider) Where do alerts go, and can I add another address?

To contact@ plus every address in /super → Settings → Alerts to you → Also send alerts to (up to 5).

(Provider) What is in the Monday scorecard?

New sign-ups, payments, paying customers, monthly recurring revenue, sign-ups and demo requests by source, outreach numbers, and every open trial (red = nobody has signed in yet: call them).

(Provider) How do I see or change the Facebook posts?

app.metricool.com → Planner: edit, move or delete any post. To refill, ask Claude "schedule the next month of Facebook posts".

(Provider) How do I install an update?
  1. hPanel → File Manager → public_html → Upload the zip.
  2. Right-click → Extract → overwrite.
  3. Delete the zip, open the site once (the database upgrades itself), test home, kiosk, /admin, /super.

Your settings and data are never overwritten.

Advanced

I'm locked out of /super. How do I get back in?
  1. Sign in with Google.
  2. Google down: "Email me a sign-in code".
  3. Email down too: break-glass password + one recovery code.
  4. All lost: SSH to the server, in the site folder run php cron/super_password.php --recovery.
  5. Then create new recovery codes in /super → Sign-in.
All email stopped (no codes, no reports). What happened?
  1. /super → Settings → Send a test email and read the error.
  2. Most likely the Microsoft Graph client secret expired: Entra → App registrations → ClockInOut → Certificates & secrets → New secret → put it in config.php → test again.
  3. Also check the mailboxes still exist and the DNS records (page 4).
  4. While email is down, use break-glass for /super; customers' admin codes can be switched off temporarily in Settings (switch back on after).
I lost app.key. Can I get the data back?

Only with the copy in your password manager. Without it, encrypted fields (phone numbers, emails, customer database passwords, stored secrets) can't be read by anyone. That's why you keep two copies.

How do I restore one customer's data from a backup?
  1. File Manager → public_html/backups/ → open the newest good date → read MANIFEST.txt.
  2. hPanel → Databases → phpMyAdmin → select that customer's database → Import → choose its .sql.gz → Go.
  3. Customer in the shared database: import into a scratch database and copy only their rows back (ask Claude to help).
The whole server is gone. How long to be back?

About 1–3 hours with your off-site backup, config.php and app.key: new hosting → upload code → put back the two secret files → create and import the databases → logos → subdomains → cron → test → switch DNS. Step by step on page 10.

A customer wants its own separate database.
  1. hPanel → Databases → create a new empty database and user.
  2. /super → New portal → choose "Own database" and enter its details.
  3. The app installs the tables itself and keeps them upgraded with every update.
How do I change prices?
  1. Change PLAN_CATALOG in app/lib/billing.php (or ask Claude) and deploy.
  2. Create the new prices in Stripe (monthly and yearly).
  3. Paste the new price_… IDs in /super → Settings.
  4. Existing subscribers keep their old price until you move them in Stripe.
A customer paid by card but the portal still says trial.
  1. Stripe → Developers → Webhooks → your endpoint → look for failed deliveries.
  2. If the signing secret was changed, copy the new whsec_… into /super → Settings.
  3. Click Resend on the failed event; the portal turns active.
  4. Meanwhile you can set it active by hand in /super → Subscribers.
How do I test payments without real money?

Stripe Test mode → create test prices and a test webhook → paste test keys and IDs in /super → Settings → pay with card 4242 4242 4242 4242 → switch back to live keys afterwards.

How do I know the hourly job really runs?

hPanel → Cron Jobs → View Output on the hourly job. You'll see lines like prospect …: email 1 sent, report lines, or Nothing due. Errors show there too.

The prospect finder key leaked. What do I do?
  1. /super → Prospects → Replace finder key (the old key stops working immediately).
  2. claude.ai → Settings → Code → Cloud environments → Timeinout → network secret "ClockInOut prospect finder key" → paste the new key (or ask Claude to show the secure form).
The lead finder stopped adding leads.
  1. claude.ai → scheduled tasks → "ClockInOut daily prospect finder" → check the last run and its report.
  2. Report says 401: the key was replaced; update the environment secret (question above).
  3. Few leads found: normal for small states; it continues with the next state.
A customer deleted their portal (or I did) by mistake.

Within 30 days: /super → Deleted portals (bottom of Subscribers) → Restore. After that: restore from backups/deleted/ or the daily backup.

A customer wants all their data exported.

Reports and Activity log export to CSV for any period. For a complete copy: their database file from the backup folder (own-database customers), or ask Claude to extract their rows from the shared database.

Can one customer ever see another customer's data?

No. Each request is tied to one portal; every lookup checks the record belongs to that portal; logins only work on their own portal. This was tested in the November security review (page 9).

The site shows an error page after an update.
  1. Extract the previous zip over public_html (rollback).
  2. To see the real error: set 'debug' => true in config.php, reload, read the message, set it back to false immediately.
Where do I find any password or key?

In your password manager. The private Credentials and recovery register page lists every account and secret, where it lives, and how to recover it, without the values themselves.

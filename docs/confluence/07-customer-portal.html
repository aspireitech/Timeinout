What your customers see and do, from sign-up to the kiosk at their door.

1. Sign up (self-service, 1 minute)

  1. Business name, industry (sets the kiosk buttons and words), state and city.
  2. Address: suggested automatically; "choose your own" link to change it.
  3. Owner name, email, password (live checklist: 10+ characters, upper and lower case, a number).
  4. The portal is live at once at clockinoutapp.com/s/name; the welcome email also shows name.clockinoutapp.com "live within 24 hours" (in red) until you create the subdomain.
  5. Protection: a hidden bot trap and a limit of 3 sign-ups per hour (10 per day) per network and 2 per email per day.

2. Admin dashboard

Who is on site now, sign-ins today, staff on site, material pickups. The sidebar holds every function.

3. Add people

  • Students & guardians and Teachers & staff: add one by one, or click Import on that list.
  • Guardians: name, relationship, phone (encrypted). Adults with no guardian sign themselves in ("Self").
  1. Import → choose Students or Staff → upload Excel (.xlsx) or CSV (max 5 MB).
  2. Match the columns once (remembered for next time).
  3. Check the preview → Import. A mistake can be undone for 24 hours on that list's page.

4. The kiosk

  1. Open the portal address on a tablet at the door and sign in once with a staff login. Add it to the home screen.
  2. A person taps their tile (Student, Teacher/Staff, Visitor, Material pickup) → types a few letters → taps their name.
  3. Students: choose who is with them (listed guardian, Authorized relative who types a name and relationship, or Self) → Sign in / Sign out.
  4. Past days can be entered only as far back as the admin allows.

5. Attendance and reports

  • Today's attendance: live status, who brought and collected each person.
  • Reports: daily, weekly, monthly or custom range; PDF, CSV or email now.
  • Scheduled reports: emailed automatically at the portal's report hour (default 7:00 local) — daily (yesterday), weekly on the chosen weekday, monthly on the 1st — only the ones ticked in settings.

6. Settings, users, materials, billing

  • Branding & settings: name, logo, colors, welcome text, time zone, past-day limit, "Always offer Authorized relative", "Open kiosk without login" (keep off unless the link is only on your own device), report recipients and schedule.
  • Users: add admins and kiosk staff, reset passwords, change your own password.
  • Pickup materials: items handed out at the kiosk (folders, books…).
  • Billing: choose and pay a plan (page 5).

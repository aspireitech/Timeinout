What your customers see and do, from sign-up to the kiosk at their door.

1. Sign up (self-service, 1 minute)

  1. Business name, industry (sets the kiosk buttons and words), state and city.
  2. Address: suggested automatically; "choose your own" link to change it.
  3. Owner name, email, password (live checklist: 10+ characters, upper and lower case, a number).
  4. The portal is live at once at clockinoutapp.com/s/name; the welcome email also shows name.clockinoutapp.com "live within 24 hours" (in red) until you create the subdomain.
  5. Protection: a hidden bot trap and a limit of 3 sign-ups per hour (10 per day) per network and 2 per email per day.

2. Admin dashboard

Who is on site now, sign-ins today, staff on site, material pickups. The sidebar holds every function.

3. Add people

  • Students & guardians and Teachers & staff: add one by one, or click Import on that list.
  • Guardians: name, relationship, phone (encrypted). Adults with no guardian sign themselves in ("Self").
  1. Import → choose Students or Staff → upload Excel (.xlsx) or CSV (max 5 MB).
  2. Match the columns once (remembered for next time).
  3. Check the preview → Import. A mistake can be undone for 24 hours on that list's page.

4. The kiosk

  1. Open the portal address on a tablet at the door and sign in once with a staff login. Add it to the home screen.
  2. A person taps their tile (Student, Teacher/Staff, Visitor, Material pickup) → types a few letters → taps their name.
  3. Students: choose who is with them (listed guardian, Authorized relative who types a name and relationship, or Self) → Sign in / Sign out.
  4. Past days can be entered only as far back as the admin allows.

5. Attendance and reports

  • Today's attendance: live status, who brought and collected each person.
  • Reports: daily, weekly, monthly or custom range; PDF, CSV or email now.
  • Scheduled reports: emailed automatically at the portal's report hour (default 7:00 local) — daily (yesterday), weekly on the chosen weekday, monthly on the 1st — only the ones ticked in settings.

6. Settings, users, materials, billing

  • Branding & settings: name, logo, colors, welcome text, time zone, past-day limit, "Always offer Authorized relative", "Open kiosk without login" (keep off unless the link is only on your own device), report recipients and schedule.
  • Users: add admins and kiosk staff, reset passwords, change your own password.
  • Pickup materials: items handed out at the kiosk (folders, books…).
  • Billing: choose and pay a plan (page 5).

7. Features & add-ons (optional, off until the admin turns them on)

Admin → Features & add-ons. Free on every plan: Emergency roll call, QR badges and Missed sign-out reminders. One add-on pack per industry for $19/month (Safe Pickup for schools and tutoring, Staff Hours for everyone else) and Premium Support $99/month; a customer only ever sees the pack for its own industry.

Emergency roll call

  1. Admin → Emergency roll call lists everyone signed in now.
  2. Tick each person as counted; Show missing only; Print.

QR badges and check-in method

  1. Admin → QR badges: print cards, email staff their badge and families their child's badge link, New badge cancels a lost one.
  2. Check-in method, separately for the main list (e.g. Students) and staff: Tap name or scan badge (default), Scan badge only, or Tap name only. The names follow the customer's industry words.
  3. For Scan badge only, set a 4–8 digit front-desk code: a person who forgot their badge taps Forgot my badge? and staff type the code (name search opens for 2 minutes; 5 wrong codes lock it for 15 minutes). The server refuses sign-ins without a badge, so it cannot be bypassed.
  4. At the kiosk: Scan my badge uses the tablet camera; USB or Bluetooth badge scanners work too. Pickup tiles and visitors are never restricted.

Missed sign-out reminders (free)

For people who sign in but forget to sign out. Admin → Features & add-ons → tick Missed sign-out reminders → choose who gets them → Save features.

  1. Who gets the reminder: Staff and parents, Staff only, or Parents and guardians only.
  2. Working days only (on by default): nothing at weekends; Monday's email covers Friday to Sunday.
  3. When: the next morning at the portal's report hour (7:00 by default, in the portal's time zone), from reports@. Replies go to the portal's first report recipient (or the owner).
  4. Who is emailed: the staff member (email on their staff record), or the parent/guardian who did the drop-off (email on the guardian record). Authorized relatives, visitors and people without an email are not emailed. One reminder per person per day.
  5. Families can stop them with the link at the bottom of their email.
  6. No extra email for the admin: the daily report (Admin → Reports → daily email on) lists everyone not signed out, with "reminder emailed", "no email on file", "family stopped reminders", and "3 times in 30 days" for people who keep forgetting.
  7. Closing-time check (optional): enter your closing time. Within the hour after it, if anyone is still signed in today, the report recipients get one email listing them, with who brought each child and their phone number. Nothing is sent when everyone has left.
  8. Nothing is ever signed out automatically: the admin corrects times in Today's attendance, so hours and pickup records stay true.

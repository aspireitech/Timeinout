What ClockInOut is

A tablet at the front door where people sign in and out in a few taps. Owners see live attendance and get reports by email. Every customer (daycare, tutoring center, clinic, office, gym, crew…) gets its own portal at name.clockinoutapp.com. One copy of the code serves every customer.

The 3 secrets you must never lose

  1. app/keys/app.key on the server: decrypts phone numbers, emails and every stored secret. Lost key = encrypted data is gone. Keep 2 copies in your password manager.
  2. app/config.php on the server: database login, domain, mail settings, cron key.
  3. Provider recovery codes (10 codes shown once in /super → Sign-in): your last way into /super.

How these pages stay current

The source of truth is docs/MASTER_GUIDE.md in the code. Every change to the product updates that guide in the same commit, then these Confluence pages and the offline copy are refreshed from it. Each page shows its last update date in Confluence's page history.

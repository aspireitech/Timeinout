1. Trial follow-up

  1. Day 1+, no own people yet: "Add your people (we can do it for you)".
  2. Day 2+, nobody signed in yet: "Put your kiosk at the door" (3 steps).
  3. 2 days left, then last day: "Choose a plan" (with the optional promotion line from /super → Settings).

Each email once, at most one a day, never to paying customers or the demo.

2. Alerts to you

New sign-up · new card customer · payment received · ACTION: payment failed · cancellation · demo request · "chose a plan, send invoice" · subdomain to-do · trial with no check-ins after 2 days · trial ends tomorrow · feature request · suspicious sign-in report · recovery code or break-glass used. Sent to contact@ and every address in "Also send alerts to".

3. Weekly scorecard (Monday 8:00)

New sign-ups, payments, paying customers, monthly recurring revenue, sign-ups and demo requests by source, outreach numbers (added, emailed, replied, demos, signed up, unsubscribed), and every open trial (red = no check-ins yet: call them).

4. Outreach to prospects

Settings (one time)

  1. /super → Prospects → your name, phone, business postal address (required by US law), emails per day (20), time zone → tick Send outreach emails automatically → Save.

What each lead receives

  1. Email 1, written for their kind of business (childcare/tutoring/schools, clinics and dental, offices/retail/restaurants, gyms, construction and field crews) with a matching banner. Example subject: "Smile Bright Dental: staff hours without the paper timesheet".
  2. Follow-up 4 days later.
  3. Last note 5 days after that, then status "No answer".

Sent from contact@ in the lead's own business hours (by state): Tue–Thu 9–12 and 13–15, Mon 10–12 and 13–15, Fri 9–12; at most 4 per hour and 20 per day. It stops automatically on sign-up, demo request or unsubscribe. You set Replied or Bounced. Every email has an unsubscribe link and your postal address. Preview any email with the Preview link on each row.

Adding leads by hand

Paste one per line: Organization, First name, Email, City, State, Type, Website, Phone (Type: clinic, dental, daycare, tutoring, school, office, gym, construction, retail, restaurant, other). District and government addresses are skipped automatically.

5. Daily lead finder (Claude)

  • What: a Claude scheduled task named "ClockInOut daily prospect finder", every day at 5:47 AM Utah time.
  • Where it searches: one state per day, rotating through Utah, Idaho, Nevada, Colorado, Wyoming, Montana, Washington, Oregon, California, Alaska, Hawaii.
  • Who: small businesses (about 5–150 staff): clinics and dental, daycares, tutoring, offices, gyms, construction and trades. Only emails published on the business's own website.
  • How it delivers: posts to https://clockinoutapp.com/api/prospects with the finder key (header X-Api-Key).
  • Runs in: Claude cloud environment "Timeinout" (claude.ai → Settings → Code → Cloud environments), which has web access and holds the key as a protected network secret.

Change or pause it

  1. Open a Claude Code session and ask "pause the daily prospect finder" or "change the states to …".
  2. Or claude.ai → scheduled tasks → ClockInOut daily prospect finder → disable.

Replace the finder key (if it ever leaks)

  1. /super → Prospects → Replace finder key → the new key is shown once.
  2. claude.ai → Settings → Code → Cloud environments → Timeinout → network secrets → "ClockInOut prospect finder key" → replace the value (or ask Claude to show the secure form).

6. Facebook posts (Metricool)

  • Metricool brand "ClockinOut" connected to the ClockInOut Facebook page; time zone America/Denver.
  • 30 posts scheduled at 10:00 AM daily (Nov 13 – Dec 12, 2026): industry tips, feature highlights, polls, pricing; each links to the matching page with ?src=facebook so sign-ups show "via facebook".
  • See or edit: app.metricool.com → Planner.
  • Refill monthly: around the 8th, ask Claude "schedule the next month of Facebook posts" (Claude reads the best-performing posts first).
  • LinkedIn and Google Business Profile need a paid Metricool plan.

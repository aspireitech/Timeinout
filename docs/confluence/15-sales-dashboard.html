Everything you need to see how outreach and the website turn into customers, and who to call today. Open it at clockinoutapp.com/super/sales (button Sales dashboard in the provider console). Screenshots use made-up example data.

Every number is clickable. Click any card, ring, funnel row, chart bar or table number to open the list of people or visits behind it, for the same dates, with a Download CSV button.

1. Pick the period

  1. Click Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, This month, Last month or This year.
  2. Or type your own dates in the two date boxes, choose By day, By week or By month, and click Apply.
  3. Times follow your outreach time zone (America/Denver). Arrows ▲▼ compare with the same number of days just before.

3. Call today (hot list)

Prospects who clicked a link in the last 7 days and have not replied. Those who looked at the free-trial or demo page are listed first. A two-minute phone call ("I saw you had a look, any questions?") converts far better than another email. After the call, set the prospect to Replied or Booked demo in Prospects.

The same list arrives by email every weekday at 8:00 (your time), together with yesterday's visits, demo requests, sign-ups, outreach emails and replies. It goes to contact@ and to the extra alert addresses in /super → Settings.

4. Website traffic

Visits are counted on our own server, so ad blockers do not hide them. One visit = one person per day (no tracking cookie; bots and link previews are left out; your own /super visits do not count).

  • Visits, share on a phone, demo requests, sign-ups and Visit → lead (a good rate is 2–5%).
  • Where visitors land: the first page of each visit.
  • Where visitors come from: google, facebook, chatgpt, outreach, direct, or any ?src= tag, with each source's demo requests, sign-ups and conversion. Click a number to see the visits or leads.

Tag every link you share with ?src=, e.g. clockinoutapp.com/?src=chamber, ?src=capterra, ?src=google-profile, or use the short links /facebook, /linkedin, /email, /friend. The first source a person came from is remembered for 90 days, so a sign-up days later is still credited to it.

5. Funnel, trends and who answers

  • Funnel rings and bars: emailed → delivered → clicked → replied → demo → signed up.
  • Over time: one small chart per measure. Hover a bar for its number; click it for that day's list.
  • Where every prospect stands today: waiting, in the sequence, no answer, interested, stopped.
  • Which businesses answer? by type of business and by state. Put more effort into the rows at the top of "Interested"; tell Claude to shift the daily finder towards them.

7. How tracking works (technical)

WhatHow
ClicksLinks in outreach emails go through /r?t=<token>&s=<step>&to=signup|demo|home, which records the click and opens a fixed page (never any other address). "home" opens the landing page for the prospect's business type, e.g. /for/dental.
ConversionsA demo request or sign-up from the same email address or organization, or from the browser that clicked the outreach link (cookie cio_p, 90 days), marks the prospect.
Replies and bouncesRead hourly from the contact inbox (page 4, "Outreach on Microsoft 365").
DataTables prospect_events (one dated row per step), prospect_sends (one row per email with the mail server's answer), site_visits (kept 400 days). Dates before 16 Nov 2026 were estimated from the email schedule.

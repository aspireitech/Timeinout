TimeInOut

A simple, colorful check-in portal for schools, daycares and after-school programs. Students sign in and out with the parent who drops them off, teachers clock in and out, families pick up materials, and the owner gets daily, weekly and monthly reports. Each subscriber signs up and gets their own branded portal at name.abc.com straight away.

Who uses it

Three kiosk flows and one admin portal

Student

Sign in / sign out with a guardian

  1. Pick the day
  2. Type a few letters, tap the name
  3. Tap Father, Mother or another guardian. Only that student's guardians appear.
  4. Sign In (drop-off) or Sign Out (pick-up)
Teacher / Staff

Start and end the day

  1. Pick the day
  2. Type a few letters, tap the name
  3. Sign In or Sign Out. Hours are totalled in reports.
Material pickup

Collect homework, books, uniforms

  1. Find the student
  2. Tap the guardian picking up
  3. Tick the items, or type something else
  4. Confirm pickup
Owner / Admin

Run the portal

  1. Upload students, parents and teachers from CSV
  2. See who is on site now
  3. Log, reports and CSV export
  4. Logo, colors, welcome text, own domain
Key decision

How "provisioning a new customer" works

The original idea was to copy all the files and a fresh database into a new folder for every customer. Most shared hosts don't let PHP create databases or subdomains, and every fix would then have to be copied many times.

This build gets the same result in under a second: sign-up inserts a tenant row, the owner's login, default pickup items and optional sample people. A wildcard DNS record *.abc.com sends every subdomain to the same code, which reads the hostname and loads that customer's data and branding. Custom domains are a CNAME plus a domain alias. Until wildcard DNS is on, every portal also works at abc.com/s/name.

Go live

Testing on your hosting today

  1. Upload the project. Point abc.com at the public/ folder, or upload everything into public_html.
  2. Create a MySQL database in cPanel and import database/schema.sql in phpMyAdmin.
  3. Copy app/config.sample.php to app/config.php. Fill in the database, base_domain, your owner login, mail settings and a cron key.
  4. Open abc.com, click Start free trial, and tick the sample data box.
  5. Log in, open the kiosk on a tablet or phone, and try all three flows.
  6. Add an hourly cron job: php cron/send_reports.php.
  7. Later, add a wildcard DNS record *.abc.com and a wildcard subdomain, then set tenant_url_mode to subdomain.
